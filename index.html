Never miss a Formula 1® session again.

Race Weekend keeps every Formula 1® Grand Prix weekend in one place, with schedules, countdowns, circuit information and sessions.

Whether you’re checking when qualifying starts or following the race weekend from anywhere, the app makes it easy to stay up to date.

Features

Everything for the weekend

01

Race weekend timeline

See the season at a glance and quickly understand what’s coming next.

02

Live countdown

Know exactly how long remains until the next session begins.

03

Complete schedules

Practice, Sprint, Qualifying and Race sessions in one clear view.

04

Circuit information

Explore circuit details and track maps for each Grand Prix.

05

Championship standings

Keep up with driver and constructor standings throughout the season.
