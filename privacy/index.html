Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Race Weekend (“the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, or share personal data.

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • No personal data is sent to the developer’s servers.

2. Data Storage

App preferences and other locally stored data, if any, are stored on your device.

  • They remain on your device.
  • They are not accessible to the developer.
  • They are removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to iOS behaviour.

3. Third-Party Services

Race Weekend does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store and platform analytics through Apple’s own services. Those services are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

4. Data Security

Because the app does not collect personal data and app preferences remain on your device, their security primarily depends on the security of your device and operating system.

5. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data, no child-specific personal information is collected by the developer.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page together with an updated effective date.
